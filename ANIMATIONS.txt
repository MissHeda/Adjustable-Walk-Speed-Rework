=======================================================================
 ADJUSTABLE WALK SPEED - REWORK
 Animation reference
=======================================================================

Everything below can be pasted straight into an animation box in the CBA
settings - the boxes take a comma separated list, and capitalisation does
not matter.


HOW AN ANIMATION NAME IS BUILT
------------------------------

Six segments, always in this order:

  1  action      Amov moved          Aidl idle           Ainv inventory
                 Aadj adjusted       Aswm swim           Assw surface swim
                 Absw bottom swim    Adve dive           Asdv surface dive
                 Abdv bottom dive
  2  pose        Perc upright        Pknl crouched       Ppne prone
  3  movement    Mstp still          Mwlk walk           Mrun jog
                 Meva run            Mspr sprint         Mtac tactical
  4  stance      Slow lowered        Sras raised         Snon none
                 Sopt optics
  5  weapon      Wrfl rifle          Wpst pistol         Wlnr launcher
                 Wbin binoculars     Wnon unarmed
  6  direction   Df forward          Db back             Dl left
                 Dr right            Dfl forward left    Dfr forward right
                 Dbl back left       Dbr back right      Dnon none

  AmovPercMwlkSlowWrflDf
  moved   upright   walking   lowered   rifle   forwards

A * stands for the rest of a name, so AmovPercMrun* is every jogging
animation there is. Wildcards work in the whitelist, the blacklist and
the per-animation speed boxes.

To find the name of one exact animation: switch Debug on in the settings,
start a singleplayer mission, do the move, and press Esc at the moment you
want. Time stops, and that animation is the newest name in the list Debug
puts on your clipboard.
